IntervalWorkout

Android · coming soon to Google Play

An interval timer for HIIT, Tabata, circuits and any workout built from work and rest. Build a timer once, then start it with one tap.

Coming soon to Google Play Support

Made for the middle of a set

Start from a classic

Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP and 40/20 HIIT are ready the first time you open the app. Use one as is, or as the starting point for your own.

Easy to read

A large progress ring and countdown you can see from across the room. Terracotta means work, sage means rest.

Cues you can hear and feel

Countdown beeps in the last seconds of each interval, vibration at every phase change, and an optional "Get ready" lead-in.

Timing you can trust

Lock your phone or switch apps and the timer stays accurate. When you come back, it's on the right interval.

Circuits your way

Give a circuit as many work and rest intervals as you need and set how many times it repeats. Totals update as you build.

See your progress

Stats chart your training over time, compare it with the previous period and track a weekly goal of 150 minutes.

Private and offline

No account and no sign-up. Your timers and workouts stay on your device, and the app works without a connection.
