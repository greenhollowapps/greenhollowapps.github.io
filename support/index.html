Support

Questions, bug reports and ideas all go straight to the developer.

Contact us

Email support@greenhollowapps.com. We read every message.

To help us sort it out quickly, please include:

  • the app's name and version (shown in your device's app settings)
  • your phone model and Android or iOS version
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can

For purchase questions, include the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt. Never send us your password or payment details.

IntervalWorkout

How do I remove ads?

Open the menu and choose Remove ads. It's a one-time purchase through Google Play, and the app then shows no ads and makes no ad requests. Ads never appear during a workout.

I bought "Remove ads" but I'm seeing ads again.

Open the menu and choose Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to Google Play with the same Google account you bought it with. If ads still show, email us with your Google Play order number.

Will the timer keep running if I lock my phone?

Yes. The timer is anchored to the clock, not the screen, so it stays accurate when the screen is off or you switch apps. A notification shows the running workout. If cues stop while the screen is off, check that notifications are allowed for IntervalWorkout and that battery optimisation isn't restricting it.

I can't hear the countdown beeps.

Check the mute button on the workout screen, the volume in Options, and your phone's media volume. Vibration cues can be turned on in Options as well.

How do I reorder my timers?

Press and hold a timer card, then drag it into place.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Go to Options → Advertising privacy choices (shown where it applies, such as the EEA and UK). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

How do I delete my workout history?

Use Options → Reset progress. Everything the app stores is on your device, so uninstalling removes it too. We don't hold any of your data.

Will my timers move to a new phone?

Your timers and history live only on your device. If your phone backs up app data (for example Android backup), they may be restored with it. Your "Remove ads" purchase can always be restored with Restore purchases.

Flickm

How do I restore my skins on a new device?

Go to Options → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought them with. Skins are restored from the store. Power-ups, continues and rewinds are consumable and can't be restored.

I bought something and didn't receive it.

Close and reopen the game, and make sure you have a connection so the store can confirm the purchase. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with your Google Play or App Store order number.

How do I get the pre-registration reward?

Pre-register on Google Play before launch, then install the game with the same Google account. The Jelly bubbles skin is granted when the game is released. See the reward terms for details.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. For Google Play, see Google Play refunds. For the App Store, use Report a Problem.

Do skins make the game easier?

No. Skins change how your flicks and pops look. They don't change the game.

Can I make the game easier to see or calmer to play?

Yes. Options includes colour symbols for telling colours apart, an aim guide, reduced motion, and separate sound, music and vibration settings.

How do I start over?

Options → Reset progress clears your levels, stars, scores and the tutorial. Your purchases and settings are kept.

Does Flickm have ads?

No. Flickm has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Privacy and legal

Neither app has accounts or servers, so we hold no personal data about you. See the privacy policies for the details.
